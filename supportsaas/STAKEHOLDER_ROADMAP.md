## ✅ Phase 1: MVP (Completed & Verified)
*Current Milestone: Course Certification Ready*

The MVP focuses on a high-fidelity frontend coupled with a robust backend-as-a-service (Supabase) to demonstrate full-stack architecture.

*   **Architecture**: React 19 + Vite for ultra-fast performance.
*   **Authentication**: Secure User/Agent system with `AuthContext` and Protected Routing.
*   **Landing Page**: "Apple-Style" premium design featuring 10+ sections (USP, Pricing, FAQ, Security, etc.) designed for conversion.
*   **Ticketing Kernel**: Real-time ticket submission and retrieval system using Supabase Postgres Realtime.
*   **Security Documentation**: Comprehensive Security & Hackers' Playbook already established for future hardening.

---

## 🏃 Phase 2: Operational Excellence (Upcoming - Q1 2026)
*Focus: UX Polish & Data Utility*

Moving from "Functional" to "Efficient".

*   **Global Ticket Search**: Implementation of fuzzy search logic to handle thousands of support entries.
*   **Ticket Lifecycle Management**: Formalizing "Open → In Progress → Resolved" states with administrative controls.
*   **Performance Dashboards**: Real-time calculation of response times and CSAT (Customer Satisfaction) metrics.
*   **Mobile-First Polish**: Ensuring the complex CRM dashboard feels native on mobile browsers.

---

## 🧠 Phase 3: Intelligence & Automation (Future Scope - Q2 2026)
*Potential Investor Milestone: AI Differentiation*

Leveraging AI to reduce human agent overhead by 40%.

*   **AI Smart Suggestions**: Using LLMs to suggest responses based on historical ticket data.
*   **Automated Routing**: Intelligently assigning tickets to the right agent based on priority and skill set.
*   **Proactive Help**: Predicting common user issues before they submit a ticket.

---

## 🌐 Phase 4: Ecosystem & Enterprise Scale (Long-term)
*Focus: Market Domination & Compliance*

*   **Omnichannel Integration**: Connectivity with WhatsApp, Slack, and Zendesk.
*   **Multi-Tenancy**: Allowing companies to spin up their own white-labeled support portals.
*   **Compliance Suite**: Achieving ISO 27001 / SOC2 readiness for enterprise clients.
*   **Platform API**: Opening up the SupportSaaS engine for third-party developers.
